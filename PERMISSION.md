## Not covered by this permission

The following were in the original download but belong to other people, so this port does **not** include them:

- The Universal Electricity API (bundled in the original source). Replaced by NeoForge's Forge Energy.
- The VoxBox announcer voice clips, which come from Valve's Half-Life. The port's Announcement System shows the text in chat instead.
- The pulse rifle sound effects. The port uses vanilla Minecraft sounds.
- A recipe helper adapted from BuildCraft. Rewritten from scratch.
